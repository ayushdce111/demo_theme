Statutory Compliance

Labour Law & Compliance

Stay protected under all applicable employment and labour regulations

EPF & ESIC Compliance

Registration, monthly filings, employee onboarding, and dispute resolution under Employees' Provident Fund and ESIC regulations.

Contract Labour Act

Registration under the Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, contractor compliance monitoring, and principal employer obligations.

Construction Worker Welfare

Compliance under the Building & Other Construction Workers Act — registration, cess filings, and welfare benefit management.

POSH Compliance

ICC constitution, complaint mechanism, annual training programs, and reporting framework under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act.

Gratuity & Bonus

Advisory on gratuity calculation, funding, trust formation, and compliance under the Payment of Gratuity Act and Payment of Bonus Act.

HR Audit & Health Check

Comprehensive HR audit identifying gaps in policies, records, compliance filings, and documentation — with a structured remediation roadmap.

Common Questions

HR Solutions FAQs

Have more questions about our HR services? Get in touch with our team.

Contact Us
Can you set up an HR function from scratch for a new company?
Yes. We specialise in greenfield HR setups — designing the entire HR framework, policies, compliance registrations, and onboarding processes for newly formed or rapidly scaling organisations in Infrastructure and Construction.
Do you manage ongoing compliance or only provide advisory?
We offer both. Under our retainer model, we can manage monthly compliance filings (EPF, ESIC, professional tax, etc.) end-to-end. For advisory-only clients, we provide guidance, templates, and review support while your team handles execution.
Is your HR service limited to Infrastructure companies?
Our primary expertise is in Infrastructure and Construction, but we have served clients in IT, legal services, and general corporate sectors as well. Our HR expertise is transferable — while our sector depth is in Infra.
How quickly can you deploy an interim HR professional?
For interim HR roles, we typically deploy within 7–15 business days depending on the seniority level and location. We maintain a bench of experienced HR professionals available for short-notice assignments.
