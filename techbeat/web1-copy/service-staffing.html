Our Methodology

The ALP Recruitment Process

A structured 6-step process that ensures precision, speed, and quality in every hire

01
Requirement Briefing

Deep-dive consultation to understand role scope, required competencies, cultural fit, and timelines. We don't begin without clarity.

02
Talent Sourcing

Multi-channel search: our proprietary database, job portals, professional networks, referrals, and headhunting for niche roles.

03
Screening & Assessment

Technical screening, competency-based interviews, reference verification, and background checks aligned with client specifications.

04
Shortlisting & Presentation

Curated shortlist with detailed candidate profiles, assessments, and our recommendation rationale presented to the client.

05
Interview & Selection

Coordinating client interviews, collecting feedback, managing candidate communication, and facilitating the final selection process.

06
Offer & Onboarding

Offer negotiation support, joining formality guidance, and post-joining follow-up to ensure successful onboarding and retention.

Common Questions

Recruitment FAQs

Have more questions? Reach out to our recruitment team directly.

Contact Us
What is your typical turnaround time for filling a position?
For most mid-level and senior roles, we present a curated shortlist within 5–10 business days. Mass staffing and project mobilisation timelines are agreed upfront based on headcount and location. We are committed to meeting every agreed deadline.
Do you handle roles outside of Infrastructure & Construction?
Our primary specialisation is Infrastructure, Construction, and Engineering. However, we also support recruitment for IT, Legal, and general corporate functions — particularly for organisations in these sectors seeking support roles.
What is your fee structure for recruitment services?
Our fees are typically a percentage of the placed candidate's annual CTC, varying by seniority level and engagement model. For mass staffing, project-based pricing is available. All fee structures are discussed and agreed transparently before engagement begins.
Do you offer a replacement guarantee?
Yes. We offer a replacement guarantee clause within a defined tenure period (typically 30–90 days depending on the role). If the placed candidate exits within the guarantee period, we conduct a replacement search at no additional cost.
Can you handle multi-location or pan-India hiring mandates?
Absolutely. Our network of independent recruitment consultants and partner institutions enables us to source talent across India, including tier-2 and tier-3 cities and remote project sites where infrastructure work is concentrated.
