Practice Areas

Key Legal Practice Areas

Specialised legal expertise aligned with the realities of Infrastructure, Construction & HR in India

01
Employment & HR Contracts

Drafting, reviewing, and standardising offer letters, appointment letters, service agreements, NDAs, non-compete clauses, and separation agreements aligned with Indian employment law.

02
Construction & Project Contracts

Review and advisory on EPC contracts, work orders, sub-contractor agreements, scope-of-work documents, and dispute clauses for Infrastructure and Civil Engineering projects.

03
Labour Law Compliance

End-to-end compliance advisory covering the Industrial Disputes Act, Factories Act, Minimum Wages Act, Contract Labour Act, BOCW Act, and other applicable statutes.

04
POSH & Workplace Law

Constituting Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), drafting complaint procedures, conducting awareness training, and preparing annual POSH reports as mandated by law.

05
Dispute Resolution & Notices

Pre-litigation advisory, drafting legal notices, responding to show-cause notices, and supporting conciliation/arbitration proceedings to resolve workplace and contract disputes.

06
Statutory Registrations & Filings

Facilitating registrations under Shops & Establishments Act, Contract Labour Act, BOCW, Professional Tax, and other applicable state and central statutes for new and existing establishments.

Legal Services FAQs

Have a specific legal challenge? Talk to our advisor today.

Contact Us
Can you handle legal matters in states outside Delhi NCR?
Yes. Through our law firm partnership, we provide advisory services across India. For litigation-related matters requiring court appearances in other states, we coordinate with associated advocates in relevant jurisdictions to ensure seamless representation.
Do you handle individual employee legal disputes or only corporate matters?
Our primary focus is advisory for organisations — helping companies manage their legal obligations, contracts, and compliance. For individual employment disputes, we can provide guidance in the context of our broader HR consulting engagement with the relevant company.
Can you audit our existing contracts and compliance records?
Absolutely. Our Legal Compliance Audit service reviews your existing employment contracts, statutory registrations, compliance filings, and HR documentation to identify gaps, risks, and areas requiring immediate remediation — along with a prioritised action plan.
How is your legal service different from a standalone law firm?
We integrate legal advice with HR and business expertise — so when you get contract advice, it comes with an understanding of how it affects your hiring, workforce management, and operations. This holistic view prevents legal decisions that are technically correct but practically disruptive to your business.
