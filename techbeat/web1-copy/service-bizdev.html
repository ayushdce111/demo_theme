Our Methodology

The Tender Winning Process

A structured approach that maximises win probability at every stage

01
Opportunity Identification & Go/No-Go

We scan portals (GeM, CPWD, NHAI, state PWDs) and private pipelines to shortlist tenders matching your capacity. We then jointly assess bid-worthiness before committing resources.

02
Tender Document Analysis

Detailed review of NIT/RFP/EOI documents — highlighting eligibility criteria, technical requirements, evaluation methodology, and risk clauses before you commit.

03
Bid Strategy & Team Mobilisation

Developing the bid strategy — deciding JV structure, sub-contractor arrangements, key personnel CVs, and the value proposition narrative for the technical proposal.

04
Technical Proposal Preparation

Writing and compiling the technical proposal — methodology, project plan, resource deployment, quality management, and compliance documents — per client format requirements.

05
Financial Bid Structuring

Advisory on financial bid preparation — BOQ verification, rate analysis benchmarking, contingency planning, and ensuring your price is competitive yet profitable.

06
Post-Submission & Negotiation Support

Supporting you through technical clarifications, pre-bid meetings, negotiation rounds, and contract finalisation to close the award successfully.

Common Questions

BD & Tender FAQs

Have a specific tender or BD challenge? Let's talk it through.

Contact Us
Do you write the technical proposals on our behalf?
Yes. Under our full bid management service, we draft the technical proposal with your inputs, manage document compilation, and coordinate submission — acting as a seamless extension of your team while you focus on operations.
Can you help us qualify for tenders we currently don't meet the criteria for?
We provide strategic advice on building eligibility over time — through JV structuring, sub-contracting arrangements, credential building, and identifying bids where your current profile is a strong fit. We set realistic expectations and a roadmap to larger opportunities.
Which tender portals and procurement clients do you cover?
We cover major government portals including GeM, CPWD eTendering, NHAI, state PWDs, irrigation departments, smart city projects, urban local bodies, as well as private sector clients in real estate, EPC, and industrial construction.
Is there a minimum contract size you work with?
We work with companies across the spectrum — from SME contractors bidding for ₹5–50 Cr projects to mid-size EPC firms targeting ₹500+ Cr contracts. Our engagement model and fee structure are calibrated to your scale and the complexity of the bid.
