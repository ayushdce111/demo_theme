Our Legacy

ALP Group draws its vision and inspiration from the late SH. ACHCHHE LAL PRIYADARSHI (ALP), (MA, B.Ed. & LL. B.), a distinguished scholar, educationist, social reformer, passionate visionary and the voice of thousands. Though he is no longer with us, his ideals and principles continue to live on, guiding and transforming both the Group and society at large. His legacy remains a constant source of inspiration for our endeavours.

ALP Group, led by Ms. Annu Kumari (M.Sc., B.Ed.) with Mr. Guru Bandhu (MBA, LL.B.) as Chief Advisor and Independent Consultant, is a new-age HR and Legal Consulting firm with over 17 years of industry experience.

We go beyond conventional HR services by helping organizations attract, hire and retain high-quality talent efficiently and cost-effectively. Backed by a dynamic team and a strong network of independent recruitment professionals, we deliver the right candidates at the right time to meet and exceed client expectations.
