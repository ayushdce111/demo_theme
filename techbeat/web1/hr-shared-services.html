What We Offer

Comprehensive HR Support Services

A full suite of HR services covering every function — from people strategy and payroll to compliance and organisational design.

HR Policy Formulation & Effectiveness

Design and implement robust HR policies aligned with your organisation's culture, industry standards, and legal requirements — reviewed for real-world effectiveness.

Performance Management System

Build and deploy structured PMS frameworks — KRA/KPI design, appraisal cycles, bell-curve calibration, and feedback mechanisms that drive a performance culture.

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Formulation

Document, standardise, and communicate HR and administrative processes through clear, actionable SOPs that ensure consistency and reduce operational risk.

Advisory & Guidance on General Administration

Expert advisory on office administration, facilities management, vendor management, and internal governance to improve operational efficiency.

HR Shared Services Setup

Design and operationalise a centralised HR Shared Services model — from HRMS implementation and process standardisation to service delivery governance.

Employee Relations & Grievance Management

Structured frameworks for employee engagement, disciplinary proceedings, grievance redressal, and maintaining positive industrial relations.

Time Office & Payroll Management

End-to-end payroll processing — attendance management, leave tracking, payslip generation, and bank disbursements — accurate, timely, every month.

Wages & Salary Administration

Structured salary administration covering CTC design, minimum wage compliance, increment workflows, and full-and-final settlements for all employee categories.

Compensation & Benefit Module

Design comprehensive C&B structures — salary benchmarking, variable pay programmes, benefits administration, and incentive schemes tied to performance.

TDS & Income Tax Compliance

Monthly TDS calculation, Form 16 generation, investment declaration management, and liaison with finance for accurate tax reporting on employee income.

HR MIS & Analytics

Custom HR dashboards and MIS reports — headcount, attrition, payroll cost analysis, and compliance trackers for data-driven HR decision-making.

Contract Labour Administration

Manage contract workforce payroll, register maintenance, muster rolls, wage registers, and contractor compliance in line with the Contract Labour Act.

EPF (Employees' Provident Fund)

Registration, monthly challan filing, UAN generation, KYC updates, claims processing, and annual return filings in full compliance with EPF & MP Act.

ESIC (Employees' State Insurance)

ESIC registration, monthly contribution filing, IP generation, half-yearly returns, and employee claim assistance under the ESI Act, 1948.

GPA & GMC Insurance

Group Personal Accident (GPA) and Group Mediclaim (GMC) policy management — enrolment, endorsements, claims coordination, and annual renewal.

Contract Labour Management

Contractor licence registration, principal employer registration, Form V/VI filing, inspection readiness, and end-to-end contract labour compliance management.

Labour Law Compliance Audit

Periodic compliance audits across applicable labour laws — Factories Act, Shops & Establishment Act, Payment of Wages Act, Maternity Benefit Act, POSH, and more.

Statutory Register & Return Filing

Timely maintenance of all statutory registers and filing of periodic returns — monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and annual — across all applicable Acts.

Employee Engagement Programmes
