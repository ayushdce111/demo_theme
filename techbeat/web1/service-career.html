Our Process

Your Career Journey with ALP

From first contact to successful career outcome, we walk with you every step of the way — providing honest guidance, practical tools, and a strong professional network to support your growth.

Begin Your Journey
1
Initial Assessment & Discovery

We begin with a structured conversation to understand your background, goals, challenges, and aspirations — building a clear starting point for your development plan.

2
Customised Development Plan

Based on the assessment, we create a personalised roadmap — identifying gaps, recommending programs, and setting realistic milestones aligned with your goals.

3
Training & Skill Building

You participate in the recommended programs — individual sessions, group workshops, or corporate modules — with experienced facilitators providing real-world context.

4
Interview & Placement Prep

5

We connect you with opportunities through our recruitment network and continue to support you post-placement — ensuring a successful transition and smooth onboarding.

Common Questions

Career & Training FAQs

Questions about our programs or your career situation? We're here to help.

Contact Us
Are your career counselling sessions available online?
Yes. We offer both in-person and virtual career counselling sessions via video call — making our services accessible to professionals across India, including those on remote project sites.
Can you deliver corporate training at our office or project site?
Absolutely. We offer on-site corporate training delivery for Infrastructure and Construction clients. Our facilitators travel to your location — whether a Delhi NCR office, a Tier-2 city, or an active project site anywhere in India.
How long are your training programs?
Program duration varies by type: individual career counselling can be a single 2-hour session or a 4–6 week series. Corporate training modules typically run 1–3 days. Customised programs are scoped based on your objectives and availability.
Do I need to be in the Infrastructure sector to benefit from your career programs?
No. While our sector specialisation is Infrastructure and Construction, our career counselling, soft skills training, and placement support programs are open to professionals from any industry. Many of our training programs — communication, leadership, labour law — are universally applicable.
